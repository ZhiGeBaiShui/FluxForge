[b][size=5]为原版化学提供更多处理手段[/size][/b]

FluxForge 让你在物品网格中搭建液体处理路线，为原版化学提供净化、温控和成分微调工具。

[list]
[*][b]净化化学原料[/b]：降低酸、碱等液体中的化学污染和重金属污染，为后续反应准备更干净的原料。
[*][b]微调化学产物[/b]：产物制成后，如果污染影响了浓度达标，可以尝试用冷萃装置减少极少量污染，调整成分比例。
[*][b]组合处理流程[/b]：配合温控、过滤、冷萃与变速装置，设计适合自己需求的液体处理路线。
[/list]

[b][size=5]目前可以怎么玩？[/size][/b]

把容器中的液体装入发射器，启动后，液体会以“流源”的形式移动。利用 L 型转向装置和通道规划路线，让流源经过不同的处理装置，也可以搭建闭环，反复处理同一份液体。

[list]
[*][b]流源发射器[/b]：发射液体，也能接收、暂存流源，方便回收和再次使用。
[*][b]过滤装置[/b]：降低化学污染和重金属污染。低流速、高温能提高过滤效率，但高温也可能带来蒸发损失。
[*][b]升温与降温装置[/b]：调整流源温度，为处理和反应创造条件。
[*][b]冷萃装置[/b]：抽取含量最低的非零液体成分，存入装置。用于污染微调时，需要留意实际成分比例。
[*][b]变速装置[/b]：根据进入方向加速或减速，调整经过装置时的流速。
[*][b]L 型转向与直通通道[/b]：转向装置改变流向；直通管主要负责美观，让路线更直观、整齐。
[/list]

商人[b]奥雅[/b]会带着装置、商品和演示到访，逐步介绍这些玩法。

[b][size=6]English Overview[/size][/b]

[b]FluxForge — Turn your inventory into a fluid laboratory.[/b]

FluxForge adds modular liquid processing to Probably Stolen. Build routes in your inventory grid to filter, heat, cool and extract components from liquids used in vanilla chemistry.

[b][size=5]What does it help with?[/size][/b]

[list]
[*][b]Cleaner ingredients[/b]: reduce chemical and heavy-metal contamination in liquids such as acids and bases before using them in further reactions.
[*][b]Fine-tuning products[/b]: when contamination prevents a finished product from meeting a concentration target, the extraction device may help remove tiny amounts of contamination and adjust its composition.
[*][b]Custom processing routes[/b]: combine temperature control, filtration, extraction and speed control to suit your materials.
[/list]

[b][size=5]Current gameplay[/size][/b]

Load liquid from a container into an emitter and activate it. The liquid travels through the grid as a moving flow. Use turns to guide it through processing devices, or build a closed loop for repeated treatment.

[list]
[*][b]Emitter[/b]: launches liquid and can also receive and store incoming flows.
[*][b]Filter[/b]: reduces chemical and heavy-metal contamination. Slower flows and higher temperatures improve filtration, while excessive heat can cause evaporation losses.
[*][b]Heater / Cooler[/b]: changes flow temperature.
[*][b]Cold Extraction Device[/b]: extracts the smallest non-zero liquid component into its own storage. Check the mixture before using it to remove contaminants; it does not let you freely select a component.
[*][b]Speed Controller[/b]: speeds up or slows down a flow depending on its entry direction.
[*][b]L-shaped Turn / Straight Channel[/b]: turns redirect flows; straight channels are primarily decorative and make routes easier to follow.
[/list]

A new merchant, [b]奥雅[/b], visits with devices, goods and demonstrations to introduce the system.

[b][size=5]Installation[/size][/b]

Requires [b]Probably Stolen 0.46[/b] and the game's [b]built-in ModLoader[/b].

[list=1]
[*]Extract the FluxForge folder into the game's Mods folder.
[*]Check that Mods/FluxForge/ contains FluxForge.dll, manifest.xml and the Textures folder.
[*]Launch the game and check that the mod has loaded.
[/list]

This is a public beta. Back up your save before testing.
